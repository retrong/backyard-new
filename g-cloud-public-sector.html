Public Sector & G-Cloud 15 Digital Services

Backyard Technologies is an accredited supplier on the G-Cloud 15 framework, delivering AI-driven data analytics, cloud migration and digital transformation for local councils, unitary authorities and the wider UK public sector. As a G-Cloud supplier, we can be engaged directly by public sector buyers without a full OJEU-style tender, giving councils and government bodies a fast, compliant route to procure cloud, data and AI capability.

We work with buyers on local government cloud migration, AI for public sector service delivery, NHS non-clinical data analytics, and net-zero reporting systems — built around the scope, users, security and data residency requirements that public sector procurement teams are required to evaluate.

Built For The Local Government Reorganisation

By April 2028, 21 areas of England will move from a two-tier council structure to larger unitary authorities — the biggest shake-up of local government in decades. Newly formed unitary councils face tight deadlines to consolidate legacy systems, migrate data and stand up compliant digital services. G-Cloud 15 gives them a route to buy that capability quickly, without a full tender process.

Rapid onboarding for new unitary councils

Structured onboarding designed to meet LGR transition deadlines without disrupting live services.

Legacy system consolidation & data migration

Migrating and reconciling data across merging authorities with clear governance and audit trails.

Compliant, audited cloud transition

Cloud migration delivered against public sector security, data residency and compliance obligations.

Net-zero & SME support platforms

Data and reporting systems that support councils' net-zero commitments and local SME programmes.

Pricing

G-Cloud 15 requires transparent, published pricing. Our full day-rate card is available on our Digital Marketplace listing and on request — we don't publish estimated figures here, as procurement teams should work from the current published rate card.

  • Is this a clinical system?

    No. G-Cloud 15 sits under the non-clinical software and services pillar of NHS England's procurement framework. Our G-Cloud listing covers non-clinical data, analytics and cloud services; any work involving clinical systems is scoped and discussed separately against the relevant clinical safety standards.

  • How quickly can we procure via G-Cloud 15?

    Buyers can engage us directly through the Digital Marketplace without running a full tender. We aim to respond to buyer enquiries within 24 hours.

  • Where is our data hosted?

    Data is hosted in the UK. Full data location, residency and security detail is set out in each service definition, available on request.
