MHINO CareOS: Financial Operating System for UK Care

MHINO becomes the "Financial Operating System for UK Care" – bridging the gap between Local Authority funding, care home compliance, and patient wellness.

A unified platform that manages the full lifecycle of care funding, one click CQC evidence mapper and monitoring out patient wellness and eMAR

Connecting funding, compliance, and care delivery in one transparent and accountable system.

Core Platform Features

MHINO CareOS provides a comprehensive suite of capabilities designed specifically for the UK care sector, ensuring transparency, compliance, and quality outcomes.

Local authority contracts management

Manage contracts with local authorities, ensuring compliance, transparency, and efficient allocation of resources.

One click CQC compliance

Automated compliance tracking aligned with CQC standards, including audit trails, incident management, and regulatory reporting capabilities embedded into daily operations.

Out patient and resident wellness tracking

Monitor the health and wellness of patients and residents, track care plans, and ensure timely interventions to improve outcomes.

MHINO Wallets for PHB and ISF

Manage personal health budgets (PHB) and individual service funds (ISF) through secure digital wallets, ensuring transparency, compliance, and efficient allocation of resources.

Financial transparency and accountability for LA/ICB and care home

Ensure financial transparency and accountability for Local Authorities (LA), Integrated Care Boards (ICB), and care homes through comprehensive reporting and audit capabilities.

MHINO Wallets

A secure digital wallet system that enables PHB (Personal Health Budgets), ISF (Individual Service Funds) and medication ring-fence funds across the care ecosystem while maintaining full audit, compliance trail and regulatory oversight.

Care Home Wallets

This is a G2B2P (Government to Business to Patient) management wallet that allows you to receive payment, manage operating expenses and track revenue by patient, maintaining complete financial records aligned with compliance requirements.

PHB Wallets

Enable patients and their families to manage personal health care budgets, choose service providers, and maintain control over their care spending within approved frameworks.

Key Wallet Features

  • Instant payment processing between funding authority and wallet
  • Complete transaction audit trails for regulatory compliance
  • Automated reconciliation with Local Authority systems
  • Real-time balance and spending visibility
  • Multi-signature approval workflows for high-value transactions
  • Integration with UK payment rails and banking systems
  • Integration with pharmacies for medication top-ups
