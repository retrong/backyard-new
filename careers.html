Who is a Yarder?

"A Yarder is a vetted professional who operates within Backyard's governance and project ecosystem, often within Backyard's delivery ecosystem, contributing to structured governance, led digital transformation programmes. Yarders are not part of an open talent pool. They are selected based on their ability to work within structured environments where governance, documentation, and accountability are essential."

Apply to Become a Yarder

Why Join the Yarders Network

Yarders contribute to advisory engagements, system delivery, and compliance-driven transformation programmes.

  • Work on structured, high-impact projects
  • Operate within governance-led delivery environments
  • Gain exposure to public sector and regulated engagements
  • Be part of a centralised and professional execution model

How It Works

Step 1 — Application

Submit your profile, experience, and areas of expertise.

Step 2 — Qualification

We assess technical capability, delivery discipline, and ability to operate within structured environments.

Step 3 — Alignment

Qualified Yarders are aligned to relevant engagements based on capability and project requirements.

Step 4 — Engagement

Work is delivered within Backyard's governance framework, with defined rules and accountability.
